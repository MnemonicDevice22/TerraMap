How TerraMap works

The map
Each square is a domain — an area of your life. Size shows how much weight it carries right now; position shows what sits next to what. Type a name below and send to add one.
Temperature
Squares fade as they go untouched: light within two days, mid up to nine, dark beyond. Cold means room, not debt. Opening a domain warms it.
Editing the map
Long press a square to select it, then Rename, cycle its Size, Move it, or Delete. Move puts you in placing mode — tap an empty cell to drop it.
Projects
Tap a domain to open its projects. Give one a target and it gains a thick bar and a +1. Tap +1 when you finish a version or a session; long press it to take one back.
All projects
The second tab lists every project from every domain at once, so anything lagging shows up regardless of where it is filed.
Where the data lives
On this phone only. Nothing is uploaded. Export from time to time.
